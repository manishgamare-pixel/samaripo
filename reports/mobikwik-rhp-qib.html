1. About the company (from the RHP)

One MobiKwik Systems Limited is a digital payments and lending platform with the Zaakpay payment gateway and MobiKwik UPI. It serves consumers (Buy Now Pay Later, payments) and merchants, and turned profitable in recent quarters after years of losses.

5. Analyst view

For the common investor this was a ₹14,787 lottery ticket with a 104x retail frenzy. Such issues deliver headline listing pops but come with the highest risk of sharp post-listing falls. Treat as speculative: book partial profits on listing pops and keep position size small. Read the RHP's risk section on competition from UPI giants.

6. Key risk factors (from the RHP)

  • Competition from UPI/BNPL players (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, Slice).
  • Regulatory changes in digital lending and payments.
  • High price volatility; small issue sizes attract speculation.
