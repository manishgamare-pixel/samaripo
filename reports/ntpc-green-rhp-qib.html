1. About the company (from the RHP)

NTPC Green Energy Limited (NGEL) is the renewable energy arm of NTPC — India's largest power utility. It owns and operates solar and wind assets and is building a large green hydrogen / energy-storage pipeline, backed by the government's 500 GW renewable target.

2. Object of the issue (RHP)

  • Fresh issue: ₹10,000 Cr (100% fresh) — capex for renewable capacity and debt repayment.
  • Reservation: Retail 10% · NII 15% · QIB 50% · Anchor 25% (carved from QIB).
  • Lot size: 138 shares · minimum application ₹14,904 (retail).

5. Analyst view

A quality, fully-PPA-backed renewable play with a PSU parent — attractive for a long-term investor at ₹14,904 per lot. But at the offered valuation the listing upside was limited (+3%), matching the weak QIB reading. Treat it as a fundamental hold rather than a listing-gain trade.

6. Key risk factors (from the RHP)

  • Valuation premium to listed renewable peers.
  • Dependence on the NTPC group for offtake, land and approvals.
  • Regulatory and merchant-tariff exposure as capacity scales.
