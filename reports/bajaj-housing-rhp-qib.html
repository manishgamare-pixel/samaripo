1. About the company (from the RHP)

Bajaj Housing Finance Limited (BHFL) is India's second-largest housing finance company by AUM (₹97,000+ crore) and a wholly-owned subsidiary of Bajaj Finserv Ltd. It offers home loans, loan against property, and developer finance, and is the fastest-growing player in the segment with ~27% YoY AUM growth and industry-leading asset quality.

2. Object of the issue (RHP)

  • Fresh issue: ₹3,600 Cr — capital for future business growth and on-lending.
  • Offer for sale: ₹2,960 Cr — by promoter Bajaj Finserv Ltd.
  • Reservation: Retail 10% · Non-institutional 15% · QIB 50% · Anchor 25% (carved from QIB).
  • Lot size: 213 shares · minimum application amount ₹14,910 (retail).

5. Analyst view

The issue was priced at a premium to the last private round but at a discount to listed peers on P/BV, with strong growth and the best-in-class asset quality. A 209x QIB and 11x retail subscription, plus a grey-market premium of ~₹95, pointed to a strong listing, which materialised at +114%. For the retail investor the ₹14,910 lot was among the most affordable ways to buy a high-quality NBFC at the time.

6. Key risk factors (from the RHP)

  • Concentration in developer finance and wholesale lending.
  • Sensitivity to interest-rate cycles and NBFC funding costs.
  • High valuations after the strong listing — consider a hold-with-stop strategy.
