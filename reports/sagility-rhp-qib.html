1. About the company (from the RHP)

Sagility India is a healthcare-focused IT and business process management (BPM) company, serving large US health-insurance and healthcare clients. It is the Indian arm of the global Sagility platform, with most revenues in US dollars.

2. Object of the issue (RHP)

  • Structure: 100% Offer for Sale (OFS) by promoter and investors.
  • Reservation: Retail 10% · NII 15% · QIB 50% · Anchor 25% (carved from QIB).
  • Lot size: 93 shares · minimum application ₹14,880 (retail).

5. Analyst view

A steady, dollar-linked healthcare BPM business, but the issue was a full OFS with muted demand. For the common investor the ₹14,880 lot was affordable, yet the data said "no listing pop". Where subscription and GMP are weak, the rational move is to skip and watch — capital preserved is a win.

6. Key risk factors (from the RHP)

  • High client concentration (top clients form a large revenue share).
  • OFS structure — no fresh capital to the company.
  • Forex and US healthcare-policy exposure.
