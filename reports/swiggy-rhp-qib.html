1. About the company (from the RHP)

Swiggy is one of India's leading on-demand convenience platforms with two core businesses: food delivery (Swiggy) and quick-commerce (Instamart). It competes with Zomato in delivery and with Blinkit/Zepto in 10-minute commerce, and was loss-making at the PAT level at the time of the offer.

2. Object of the issue (RHP)

  • Fresh issue: ₹4,499 Cr — for Instamart expansion (dark stores, tech) and general corporate purposes.
  • Offer for sale: ₹6,828 Cr — by early investors (Accel, Norwest, Elevation, etc.).
  • Reservation: Retail 10% · NII 15% · QIB 50% · Anchor 25% (carved from QIB).
  • Lot size: 38 shares · minimum application ₹14,820 (retail).

5. Analyst view

For the common investor, the 38-share lot (₹14,820) made a big-name internet IPO affordable, but the loss-making P&L and intense quick-commerce cash burn demand caution. QIB anchor interest of ₹5,085 Cr de-risked the listing, while retail's 1.48x showed hesitation. A flat-to-modest listing was the honest outcome — a case where reading the RHP's risk factors mattered more than brand recognition.

6. Key risk factors (from the RHP)

  • Continued losses and negative free cash flow in quick-commerce.
  • Competition from Zomato, Blinkit and Zepto compresses margins.
  • No dividends; profitability timeline not assured.
