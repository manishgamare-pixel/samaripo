1. About the company (from the RHP)

Unimech Aerospace is a precision-engineering company making critical machined components and assemblies for aerospace engines, airframes and defence systems, with exports to the US and Europe and a strong, growing order book.

5. Analyst view

High-growth niche play with a strong order book, but small size equals high volatility. The 19-share lot (₹14,915) made a high-priced aerospace name affordable. Strategy for such issues: apply for the lottery, and on listing book partial profit rather than expecting every pop to extend. Re-read valuations after the +110% run — they are now rich.

6. Key risk factors (from the RHP)

  • Small revenue base — customer and order concentration.
  • Export and forex exposure.
  • Rich valuation post-listing; sharp retracement risk.
