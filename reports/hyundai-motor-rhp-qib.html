1. About the company (from the RHP)

Hyundai Motor India Limited (HMIL) is India's second-largest passenger vehicle maker and the number-one SUV manufacturer, with the largest export hub among carmakers in India. It is a wholly-owned subsidiary of Hyundai Motor Company (Korea) and the first automaker IPO in India in 20 years.

2. Object of the issue (RHP)

  • Structure: 100% Offer for Sale (OFS) by promoter — the company receives ZERO proceeds.
  • Reservation: Retail 10% · NII 15% · QIB 50% · Anchor 25% (carved from QIB).
  • Lot size: 7 shares · minimum application ₹13,720 (retail).

5. Analyst view

A financially excellent company but an expensive offer: at ₹1,960 the P/E was in the mid-20s with a ~0.5x retail subscription and near-zero GMP. The "no proceeds to the company" structure and premium valuation are the reasons. As a common investor the lot of ₹13,720 was affordable, but demand data (QIB 0.73x, retail 0.52x) correctly warned of a flat listing. Lesson: size and subscription data matter more than brand.

6. Key risk factors (from the RHP)

  • OFS structure — no fresh capital for the company.
  • Valuation premium to global peers and listed Indian auto majors.
  • Slower growth in entry-level segments and EV transition costs.
