1. About the company (from the RHP)

Vishal Mega Mart is a pan-India mass-market retail chain (grocery, apparel and general merchandise) with 600+ stores. Its "everyday low price" model, heavy private-label mix and high gross-margin retail assortment make it one of India's largest value retailers.

2. Object of the issue (RHP)

  • Structure: 100% Offer for Sale (OFS) by promoter and investors (Samson/TPG).
  • Reservation: Retail 10% · NII 15% · QIB 50% · Anchor 25% (carved from QIB).
  • Lot size: 192 shares · minimum application ₹14,976 (retail).

5. Analyst view

A high-retail-appeal, low-ticket IPO. The combination of a 14x retail subscription, a ₹14,976 minimum lot and mass-market positioning produced a +44% listing — a textbook example of the "common man" issue that Samar's IPO Market looks for. Post-listing, watch margins in grocery and same-store sales growth.

6. Key risk factors (from the RHP)

  • Low-margin grocery business with high working-capital intensity.
  • Intense competition from organised and unorganised retail.
  • OFS structure — no fresh capital to the company.
