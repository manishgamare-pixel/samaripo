How to Apply Safely

1

Use ASBA via your own bank

Log in to your bank's net-banking or your broker's app (Zerodha, Groww, Angel One, etc.). Pick the IPO and submit an ASBA application.

2

Approve the UPI mandate

Money is blocked in your own account, not paid to anyone. Approve the UPI autopay mandate when your bank app asks.

3

Wait for allotment

If allotted, shares land in your demat account on listing day. If not, your blocked amount is automatically unblocked in 1–2 days.

4

Book profit or hold

Review the listing and your own risk budget. Never invest more than you can afford to lose.

Precautionary Measures (Read Before Investing)

Security of your money comes first. These checks will keep you safe.

Never pay any website to "apply" for an IPO

No legitimate IPO charges you money on a website. The only payment path is ASBA/UPI through your bank or broker. Any site or agent asking for direct payment is a scam.

Verify the RHP on BSE/NSE

Read the Red Herring Prospectus from the exchange's official site only. Check the object of the issue, OFS vs fresh issue, and the risk factors.

Your money stays in YOUR account

With ASBA/UPI the amount is blocked, not transferred. It only moves if you are allotted shares. If anyone asks you to transfer money, it is a red flag.

Beware of "guaranteed allotment" and fake GMP tips

Grey-market premium (GMP) is unregulated speculation. Nobody can guarantee allotment or profits. Treat all GMP figures as entertainment, not advice.

Protect credentials

Never share OTPs, bank login, or demat passwords. Double-check app names, URLs, and WhatsApp numbers. SEBI-registered brokers never ask for your password.

Size the position

IPO allotment is a lottery - not guaranteed. Keep IPO money to a small part of your savings and spread across a few issues rather than all-in on one.
